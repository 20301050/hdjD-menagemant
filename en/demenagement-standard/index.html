Standard

Package 2 – Standard : Removals with movers

The right balance: you pack your boxes, we take care of the furniture and transport.

What's included

The Standard package suits most moves. You pack your boxes at your own pace; on moving day, our teams dismantle, protect, load and transport your furniture, then reassemble and set it up on arrival.

  • Free quote and advice on packing your boxes
  • Dismantling and reassembly of large furniture (beds, wardrobes, tables)
  • Furniture protection with blankets and covers
  • Careful loading and securing in the lorry
  • Transport by an experienced team
  • Unloading and placing of furniture
  • Boxes placed in the rooms you specify
  • Boxes and packing supplies available as an option (see Packing supplies)

Who is it for?

  • Couples and families moving out of a medium-sized home
  • Those who want to stay in control of their boxes and personal belongings
  • Those looking for good value for money

Your role: pack and label your boxes before moving day.
